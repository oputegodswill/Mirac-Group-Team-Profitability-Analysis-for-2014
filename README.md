1. Introduction
I analyzed sales and profitability data for Mirac Group Data Team for the year 2014. The dataset contains 369 order records with 26 fields including Order Date, Customer Name, Salesperson, Region, Product Name, Category, Payment Type, Quantity, Unit Price, Revenue, and Shipping Fee. I performed the entire analysis and visualization in Power BI and built a 3-page report - SUMMARY NOTES, DASHBOARD, and SUCCESSION - as shown above. The report was prepared for senior Executives' summit for the first quarter of 2025.
2. Objective
My objectives were:
1. To calculate total revenue $435k+ and identify top sales personnel Nancy $104k+, best performing region/city New York $67k+, peak month December $66k+, best product Beverages $119k+, and most used payment method Credit Card 39.61%.
2. To measure monthly revenue trend, quarterly payment method trend, and regional revenue split.
3. To analyze quantity purchased per product category and city revenue performance, salesperson performance, and product sales by quantity and revenue.
4. To evaluate revenue by product category, by city, by payment method, and by product.
5. To build a 3-page Power BI dashboard for senior Executives' summit with summary notes and succession planning.
